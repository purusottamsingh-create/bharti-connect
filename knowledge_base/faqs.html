Help & FAQs

Quick answers to frequently asked questions about Bharti Connect services and support.

Plans & Recharge

How do I check my remaining data?

Dial *121# and select option 1, or check the Bharti Connect app.

Does unused daily data carry forward?

No, daily allowances reset at midnight and do not carry over.

Network & 5G

How do I enable 5G on my phone?

Ensure you have a 5G plan (₹399+) and a 5G phone. Set your network type to 5G/Auto in your mobile settings.

Aarav AI Assistant

What is Aarav?

Aarav is our AI assistant available in the app to help with balance checks, billing disputes, and plan activation instantly.
