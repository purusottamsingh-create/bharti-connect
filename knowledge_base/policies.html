Our Policies

We maintain clear policies to ensure a fair and consistent experience for all Bharti Connect customers.

Fair Usage Policy (FUP)

Prepaid & Postpaid

Once your daily (prepaid) or monthly (postpaid) data limit is reached, speed is reduced to 64 Kbps. Full speed can be restored via boosters.

Refunds & Credits

Guidelines

Refunds for duplicate payments or service activation failures are processed within 7 working days. Billing errors result in account credits within 24 hours.

Number Validity

Grace Periods

0-15 Days: Incoming calls only. | 16-90 Days: Suspended. | 90+ Days: Permanent deactivation.
