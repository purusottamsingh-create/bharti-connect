Switch to Bharti Connect

Keep your existing number and switch to India's fastest network. The Mobile Number Portability (MNP) process takes just 2 working days.

Eligibility Checklist

✓ Current number must be active

✓ No porting in the last 90 days

✓ No outstanding dues with current operator

✓ Registered in your name with valid KYC

The Porting Process

1. Generate UPC

SMS PORT 10-digit number to 1900. You'll receive a code valid for 4 days.

2. KYC & SIM

Visit a store with your Aadhaar or apply online for home delivery of your new SIM.

3. Activation

Once porting is confirmed (Day 2), insert your new SIM and restart your phone.
