Your Bill Explained

Your Bharti Connect bill is a detailed record of all charges for the billing period. Learn how to read it and resolve disputes.

Common Billing Terms

Plan Charge

The fixed monthly cost of your chosen postpaid plan (e.g., ₹699).

Excess Usage

Charges for data, calls, or SMS beyond your plan's monthly limit. Data excess is ₹30/GB.

Taxes & Levies

Includes 18% GST and TRAI mandated Universal Service Obligation (USO) levy.

Payment Methods

UPI Payments

Pay via Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm by scanning the bill QR code.

Auto-Pay

Link your bank account or card in the app for automatic, on-time payments.

Net Banking

Secure payments via all major Indian banks through our portal.
